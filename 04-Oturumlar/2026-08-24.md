### Decisions Made
1. **Event logging is failure-proof** — if writing to `EventLogs` fails, the customer's business transaction still completes. Design principle: observability never blocks business flow.
2. **IP hashing with salt** — stored as salted SHA-256 (not plain hash), to prevent rainbow table attacks on IPv4 address space (4 billion exhaustive attempts).
3. **Abandoned payment timeout is 15 minutes** — validated by measuring actual hold duration (`tutmaSuresiSaniye` field), enabling future analysis of decision time distribution ("users abandon after 3 minutes" vs "12 minutes").
4. **EF behavior on partitioned tables documented, not blocked** — `RETURNING` doesn't reload generated `Seq` on partitioned tables; test adjusted to validate what actually matters (olay yazıldı mı), not internal state.
