4. **Cancellation priority rule**: Cancellation beats pending transfer; transfer dropped, payment refunded automatically if made
5. **Price snapshot renewal**: Complete refresh on transfer (not just total amount)
6. **Boarding token expiry**: Renewed on transfer (critical for forward reschedules — token must not expire on old date)
